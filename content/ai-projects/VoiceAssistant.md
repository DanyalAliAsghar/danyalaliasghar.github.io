At dinCloud, I developed a voice-assistant prototype that connected Whisper speech-to-text with GPT-4 responses. I built the path from spoken input to a conversational answer, bringing speech recognition and language generation into one workflow.

I refined prompt templates to improve conversational response quality and latency. I used the prototype to work through those two practical concerns together: how the assistant responded and how long someone had to wait for its answer.
