I built a retrieval-augmented knowledge assistant with OpenAI, LangChain, LlamaIndex, Pinecone and FastAPI. I combined embedding search with prompt chains so questions could draw on relevant material from internal documentation and policy archives.

I connected retrieval and response generation through an API, bringing document search into a question-and-answer workflow. I focused the project on a practical use of generative AI: helping people work with information already held in their documents.
